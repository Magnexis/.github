Who we are

Small enough to explore.
Focused enough to build.

Magnexis LLC is an independent software engineering and research-and-development company established in Connecticut in 2026. Our work spans developer tools, digital experiences, applied AI experimentation, systems engineering, and defensive cybersecurity research.

We operate with a simple belief: meaningful technology starts with curiosity, disciplined engineering, and a willingness to rethink familiar assumptions.

Our public portfolio includes open-source repositories and active experiments. Some work remains at the research or prototype stage; we communicate those distinctions plainly.
